# Surface: Swatch app (index.html)

Mode: Operate. Single-screen mobile-first PWA with two tabs (Combinar, Mis clásicos). Audience: the owner, at home, getting dressed with one garment in hand. Task: pick a colour → see grouped suggestions → preview top/bottom on a silhouette. Build path: code-led.

## Direction contract

THESIS: Swatch is a mercer's shade card: every colour is a flat numbered band (Nº 01–18) on bone card stock, grouped by family; choosing one pulls its companions into a matching strip. Refuses the category default of round colour dots on white cards with shadows and a bottom sheet.

OWN-WORLD: Bone card ground (#EEE7DA), lighter card stock panels, sepia printing ink for all text and hairlines, one printed gilt for ★. Flat colour bands with no radius, no shadow, no border except a hairline on pale bands; shade numbers in small tabular figures printed on the band. Serif display (Gloock) for headings and the chosen colour's name; Figtree for UI text. Hierarchy by scale alone (raise from variable-font specimen). The colour field is the control, no chrome (raise from metro tiles). State as printed marks inside the cell: ring for selected, ✓ for owned in phase 2 (raise from convention catalog). Top and bottom are hard halves; Invertir is a real Y-axis flip (raise from cassette J-card).

STORY: The owner sees their garment colour on a figure, knows instantly which of their own classics pair with it, then scans neutrals, tone-on-tone, complementary and analogous groups, and taps one to try it on the figure.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Header: serif "Swatch" left, two text tabs right. Stage card: SVG silhouette (≈120px wide) left; right, shade number + family small caps, colour name in large serif, "con <pair>", ★ classic badge, Invertir button. Below: matching strips by group, then the full shade card grid (6 columns, whole cells), and a "Color libre" picker row. 60-30-10 note at the end.

FORM: Mercer's thread shade card, candidate 6 of 7 on the ordered list. seed:d2a8e478474950a8
